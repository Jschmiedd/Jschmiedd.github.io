FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the simulator free?

Yes. Thrust, speed, flight time, acceleration and safety alerts are free, with no sign-up. Premium adds prototype frames, the Quad Speeder, 3D views, STL/DXF import, GPS calibration and the online garage.

How accurate are the calculated speeds?

The model is calibrated on real GPS-measured flights (121 and 126 km/h) and accounts for tilt, air density and the real battery size. With Premium you can recalibrate it on your own GPS measurement.

How is it different from eCalc?

eCalc is a reference for pure calculation. TALKTRUE FPV is a build platform: real components with prices and shop links, side-by-side comparison in a garage, dimensioned sketches, 3D models and import of your CAD files.

Can I import my drone from SolidWorks or Fusion 360?

Yes, with Premium: an STL file for the 3D view and the exact frontal area at every tilt, or a DXF drawing whose silhouette is measured automatically.
