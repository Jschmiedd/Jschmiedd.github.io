The method

The calculation method: physics, calibrated on real flights

How the calculator works: it starts from the thrust measured on the bench by each motor's manufacturer, with its reference propeller and voltage, and scales it to your propeller and your battery. It corrects it for the air density of the day (altitude, temperature, humidity, QNH) and lowers it with speed down to the propeller's pitch speed, set by the motor's rpm (KV and voltage) and the pitch. At the flying tilt, this thrust must carry the quad's weight and overcome its drag, which grows as it tilts: top speed is the point where thrust, weight and drag balance.

1

Today's air

Pressure from QNH and altitude, water vapour (Tetens), then humid-air density: thrust and drag follow the real density.

2

Thrust

Measured thrust of the chosen motor, scaled to the prop diameter, the cell count and the air density of the day.

3

Top speed

Thrust drops with speed down to the prop's pitch speed; at full throttle it carries the weight and balances the drag.

4

Drag as the wind sees it

The more the quad tilts, the more the wind sees its top. For a prototype, the area is measured on the silhouette of the full 3D model at every angle.

5

Flight time

Hover current, share of the reserve by flying style, voltage sag from internal resistance and the chemistry's discharge curve.

New · PRO · OpenFOAM CFD simulation

The power of a full CFD solution, made accessible

A CFD analysis usually takes a lot of preparation: computational domain, mesh, boundary conditions, convergence. Here everything is automatic and checked: you give the tilt and speed, and the calculation runs on our dedicated server.

🎬 Video render of your STL: optional with every CFD run, the server makes this video (1920 × 1080) from your model; watch it and download it as .mp4 from the app. New: a second render to choose from: “tubes” (preview in the Quad Speeder chapter).

🧩

Custom geometry

No generic shapes: submit the exact architecture of your frame, fuselage and battery as you modelled them.

🛡️

Validated at every step

Mesh quality check (checkMesh), several fallback meshes (up to 5 attempts, with then without boundary layers; the report says which one was kept). Convergence criterion: drag stable within 3 % over the last 100 iterations (1 % and extra extensions at maximum accuracy). Strict rejection of outliers: any anomaly raises an explicit alert.

📐

Multi-incidence runs

Up to 3 incidences per request, to the degree; speed up to 400 km/h (800 km/h for a Speeder); air box of your choice (automatic, compact or large), blockage checked. Two requests at a time; a cancelled request is not counted.

Result margin: a CFD result that carries its margin (95 %) shows a range for drag and top speed. No margin is ever made up: without an interval, the range stays “to be confirmed”.

The full step-by-step guide (launch the run, read the results, animate the air in ParaView) is in the app's user guide.

TrueFoam GUI · CFD on a Windows PC

CFD on your own PC, free and offline

TrueFoam GUI computes the airflow around a 3D model (STL file) on your PC, for OpenFOAM v2412, with no command line. It also opens runs made on the online calculator (“Files for TrueFoam GUI” zip), or hands them to the visualisation software of your choice, ParaView for example.

  • Study parameters: STL file integration, set-up of speed, tilt and atmospheric conditions.
  • Mesh generation and solving: fully automated process through the graphical interface (GUI), with no command line to type.
  • Data post-processing: drag analysis, pressure mapping, slice planes, streamlines and automatic PDF report generation.
  • Interoperability: import and centralise your CFD runs computed on the external TALKTRUE compute server (launched from the online calculator, retrieved as a zip file), directly in TrueFoam GUI.
  • Advanced physics: internal flows (pipes), hydrodynamics (flotation and waves), turbulence noise estimate and heat transfer (validation in progress).
  • Deployment: fully guided, simplified step-by-step installation.
  • Technical environment: compatible with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Docker Desktop. Automated, secure download of OpenFOAM during installation, with your consent (OpenFOAM is not bundled with TrueFoam GUI).

PRO · high-speed drone

Quad Speeder · high-speed drone

The Quad Speeder is the drone built for speed: a streamlined fuselage drawn in the app, 3D-printed, run through the wind tunnel and then CFD.

Quad Speeder

High-speed UAV: pusher or tractor configurations

  • Aerodynamic profile: "teardrop" fuselage. Drag coefficient (Cx) optimised from the fineness ratio, following the Hoerner method.
  • Propulsion architecture: integrated pusher configuration (SPEEDER V2 type) or tractor on dedicated nacelles (Blackbird-type architecture).
  • Fuselage incidence: 0° attitude in fast flight facing the wind (minimum drag) and 90° during take-off and hover.
  • STL modelling: geometric integration at nominal scale, including the battery volume and an adjustable centre of gravity (CoG).
  • Full internal integration: electronic components and antennas laid out inside the fuselage. The app flags any part that does not fit.
  • Dimensioned 2D drawings (DXF): Speeder deliverables in pusher or tractor configuration, ready to export to your CAD software.

OpenFOAM CFD of the V2V Speeder, step by step

  1. 1 · STL export

    Exported from SolidWorks, Fusion 360 or Onshape. Geometric orientation checked on 3 orthogonal views (as in a slicer).

  2. 2 · Automated meshing

    STL mesh repair and fluid domain sized to the aircraft: snappyHexMesh discretisation of about 1 million cells, progressive refinement near the walls and in the wake, 5 prismatic boundary layers on the wall.

  3. 3 · OpenFOAM solution

    simpleFoam solver, k-ω SST turbulence model with Spalding wall function, until the drag stabilises. Without convergence, the run is extended once (+50 % iterations), twice at maximum accuracy, then the result is explicitly flagged "not converged".

  4. 4 · Built-in post-processing

    S·Cx drag, equivalent drag coefficient Cx, lift, correlation with the analytical model and 3D pressure map.

  5. 5 · ParaView export

    Surface data, cut planes and streamlines in VTK format, with an automation macro to visualise the flow.

Where the air pushes, where it lets go

The pressure on every surface shows where the drag comes from: red, the air hits the drone; blue, it is sucked away. Behind it, the wake shows the slowed air the drone drags along.

Real numbers

Fuselage into the wind: 5 times less drag

  • Flat into the wind, the V2V Speeder has only 9 cm² of drag area: an equivalent drag coefficient of 0.22, an imperfect teardrop.
  • Tilted at 45°, the same drone reaches 45 cm²: five times more. Keeping the fuselage in the wind is the key to speed.
  • Recomputed at 500 km/h by the TALKTRUE server: 8.3 and 44 cm², the drag coefficient stays almost the same.
  • The app's quick estimate, calibrated on a 5″ freestyle quad, overestimates the drag of a streamlined fuselage: CFD corrects it.

Steady RANS, without the propellers or their slipstream, wall functions: ±15 to 25 %. To be confirmed by a GPS flight, like the rest of the configurator.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the configurator free?

Yes. The full calculator (thrust, speed, flight time, acceleration), over 200 frames, safety alerts, the cart grouped by retailer and a garage of 3 builds are free with no sign-up. PRO adds the online garage of 10 builds, the wind tunnel, CFD, the prototype frame, the Quad Speeder, 3D, STL/DXF import and GPS calibration.

What is the CFD simulation?

A real simulation of the airflow around your drone, run with OpenFOAM (the open-source CFD software used in industry) from your STL file. You get the computed drag area (S·Cd), the equivalent drag coefficient, lift, a 3D pressure map and a file pack for ParaView.

Do I need to know OpenFOAM?

No. Upload your STL, pick the tilt and speed, and the compute server does everything: STL repair, domain, snappyHexMesh meshing, k-ω SST solve until convergence, checks. Nothing to install on your computer.

How long does a run take?

A few tens of minutes per tilt, depending on the model size and the queue. You can close the app: the run continues, the result waits for you in Prototype › Calcul CFD (“Mes calculs CFD”), and if you leave an e-mail address the server tells you when the results are ready.

How do I view the results in ParaView?

Every run comes with a zip for ParaView (free): drone surface, streamlines and a macro that makes the air flow. It also provides a zip for TrueFoam GUI, a study folder (CSV of S·Cx, forces, parameters), the video as .mp4 and an “Apply these results to the calculator” button that replaces the estimated drag with the CFD one. The illustrated step-by-step is in the app's user guide.

Is the CFD exact?

It is a steady (RANS) simulation without the propellers or their slipstream, with wall functions: allow ±15 to 25 %. It's ideal to compare shapes and see where drag comes from. Real speed is confirmed by a GPS flight, on which you can recalibrate the configurator.

And the virtual wind tunnel?

It's the instant estimate, with the airflow and prop slipstream illustrated. Use it to explore, then CFD to settle it.

How is it different from eCalc?

eCalc is a reference for pure calculation. TALKTRUE FPV - XRIG Configurateur is a design platform: CFD of your own model, real parts with prices and shop links, a build garage, dimensioned sketches, 3D models and import of your CAD drawings.

Who is BOB?

BOB the Racer is the yellow-canopy racing drone that inspired the TALKTRUE FPV logo: his silhouette sits at the centre of the sphere. The brand's mascot, and a real drone that flies.
