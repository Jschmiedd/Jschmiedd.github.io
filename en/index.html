Quad Speeder

High-speed UAV: pusher or tractor configurations

  • Aerodynamic profile: "teardrop" fuselage. Drag coefficient (Cx) optimised from fineness ratio, following the Hoerner method.
  • Propulsion architecture: integrated pusher configuration (SPEEDER V2 type) or tractor on dedicated nacelles (Blackbird-type architecture).
  • Fuselage incidence: 0° attitude in fast flight facing the wind (minimum drag) and 90° during take-off / hover.
  • STL modelling: geometry at nominal scale including the battery volume and an adjustable centre of gravity (CoG).

Virtual wind tunnel

The instant estimate, ahead of the CFD solution

  • Aerodynamic equilibrium: instant computation of the equilibrium tilt, drag, aerodynamic power absorbed and required thrust.
  • Dynamic rotor modelling: wake of the spinning propellers included, thrust loss from interaction with the frame (wake effect) and front / rear load split.
  • Instant generation: real-time rendering to explore concepts; final validation by solving the CFD equations.

The wind tunnel module applies the simulator's analytical formulations, calibrated on real flight data. Streamlines and particles illustrate the flow. The CFD run, for its part, numerically solves the Navier-Stokes equations around your STL mesh.

New · PRO · OpenFOAM CFD simulation

The power of a full CFD solution, made accessible

A full CFD analysis traditionally requires complex pre-processing (computational domain, mesh discretisation, boundary conditions, solver convergence). Our solution runs a fully automated and verified pipeline. Enter your operating parameters (attitude and speed): the numerical solution is offloaded and run transparently on our dedicated compute server.

  1. 1 · STL export

    Exported from SolidWorks, Fusion 360 or Onshape. Geometric orientation checked on 3 orthogonal views (as in a slicer).

  2. 2 · Automated meshing

    STL mesh repair and fluid domain sized to the aircraft: snappyHexMesh discretisation of about 1 million cells, progressive refinement near the walls and in the wake; at maximum accuracy, 5 prismatic boundary layers on the wall.

  3. 3 · OpenFOAM solution

    simpleFoam solver, k-ω SST turbulence model with Spalding wall function, until the drag stabilises. Without convergence, the run is extended (+50 % iterations), then the result is explicitly flagged "not converged".

  4. 4 · Built-in post-processing

    S·Cx drag, equivalent drag coefficient Cx, lift, correlation with the analytical model and 3D pressure map.

  5. 5 · ParaView export

    Surface data, cut planes and streamlines in VTK format, with an automation macro to visualise the flow.

What CFD reveals

Where the air pushes, where it lets go

The pressure on every surface shows where the drag comes from: red, the air hits the drone; blue, it is sucked away. Behind it, the wake shows the slowed air the drone drags along.

🎬 Video render of your STL: optional with every CFD run, the server makes this video (1920 × 1080) from your model; watch it and download it as .mp4 from the app.

Real numbers

Fuselage into the wind: 5 times less drag

  • Flat into the wind, the V2V Speeder has only 9 cm² of drag area: an equivalent drag coefficient of 0.22, an imperfect teardrop.
  • Tilted at 45°, the same drone reaches 45 cm²: five times more. Keeping the fuselage in the wind is the key to speed.
  • Recomputed at 500 km/h by the TALKTRUE server: 8.3 and 44 cm², the drag coefficient stays almost the same.
  • The app's quick estimate (fixed Cd 0.68 for an STL) overestimates the drag of a well-streamlined Speeder: CFD corrects it.

Steady RANS, without the propellers or their slipstream, wall functions: ±15 to 25 %. To be confirmed by a GPS flight, like the rest of the simulator.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the simulator free?

Yes. The full calculator (thrust, speed, flight time, acceleration), over 200 frames, safety alerts, the cart grouped by retailer and a garage of 3 builds are free with no sign-up. PRO adds the online garage of 10 builds, the wind tunnel, CFD, the prototype frame, the Quad Speeder, 3D, STL/DXF import and GPS calibration.

What is the CFD simulation?

A real simulation of the airflow around your drone, run with OpenFOAM (the open-source CFD software used in industry) from your STL file. You get the measured drag area (S·Cd), the equivalent drag coefficient, lift, a 3D pressure map and a file pack for ParaView.

Do I need to know OpenFOAM?

No. Upload your STL, pick the tilt and speed, and the compute server does everything: STL repair, domain, snappyHexMesh meshing, k-ω SST solve until convergence, checks. Nothing to install on your computer.

How long does a run take?

A few tens of minutes per tilt, depending on the model size and the queue. You can close the app: the run continues and the result waits for you in the 🌀 Calcul CFD tab.

How do I view the results in ParaView?

Every run comes with a zip for ParaView (free): drone surface, streamlines and a macro that makes the air flow. The illustrated step-by-step is in the app's user guide.

Is the CFD exact?

It is a steady (RANS) simulation without the propellers or their slipstream, with wall functions: allow ±15 to 25 %. It's ideal to compare shapes and see where drag comes from. Real speed is confirmed by a GPS flight, on which you can recalibrate the simulator.

And the virtual wind tunnel?

It's the instant estimate: same formulas as top speed, calibrated on GPS flights, with the airflow and prop slipstream illustrated. Use it to explore, then CFD to settle it.

How is it different from eCalc?

eCalc is a reference for pure calculation. TALKTRUE FPV is a design platform: CFD of your own model, real parts with prices and shop links, a build garage, dimensioned sketches, 3D models and import of your CAD drawings.
